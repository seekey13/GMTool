# GM commands

Generated by `tools/gen_docs.lua` from `core/catalog.lua`: 200 forms for the 199 commands in the server's `scripts/commands/`. `<...>` must be given, `[...]` may be left empty. GM levels are the server's own (`cmdprops.permission`).

## Player

HP, MP and TP, raising, immortality and resets for a player.

| Command | GM | Arguments | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `!hp` | 1 | <HP (number)> [Player] | Set HP. 0 kills; more than the max gives the max. A dead player is refused: raise them first. |
| `!mp` | 1 | <MP (number)> [Player] | Set MP; more than the max gives the max. |
| `!tp` | 1 | <TP (number)> [Player] | Set TP (0 to 3000). Their pet's TP is set too. |
| `!raise` | 1 | [Raise (choice)] [Player] | Send the raise menu to a dead player, who still has to take it. |
| `!immortal` | 1 | [Player] | Toggle: HP never falls below 1. A punching bag that still takes damage. Its state is the char var Immortal (!checkvar <name> Immortal). |
| `!godmode` | 1 | [Tier (choice)] | Toggle on yourself: invincible, Perfect Dodge, Regen, Refresh, +1000% HP and more. |
| `!petgodmode` | 1 |  | Toggle god mode on your pet. |
| `!pettp` | 1 | <TP (number)> | Set your pet's TP. |
| `!reset` | 1 | [Player] | Reset job ability and spell recasts to 0. It also clears YOUR debuffs, whoever is named. |
| `!homepoint` | 1 | [Player] | Send a player to their home point (revives the dead at full HP and MP). |
| `!release` | 1 | [Player] | Release a player stuck in an event or cutscene. |
